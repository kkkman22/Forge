# Folio Billing — Marriott Extension

## Bonvoy Points Multiplier

The multiplier applied to eligible charges to calculate loyalty points. Varies by membership tier: Silver 10x, Gold 15x, Platinum 20x, Titanium 25x, Ambassador 30x.

## Elite Night Credit

A qualifying night credited to a member's account toward tier qualification. Awarded for each eligible stay night regardless of rate type.

## Terms added by this sample (demonstrating additive glossary):

- **Bonvoy Points Multiplier** — tier-based point calculation factor
- **Elite Night Credit** — qualifying night toward tier status
